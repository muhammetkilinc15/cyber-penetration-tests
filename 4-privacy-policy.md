## Privacy Policy

### Challenge: Read our privacy policy.

1. Navigate to registration screen, Account > Login > Not yet a customer?
2. Register as new user
3. After redirect to login screen, login with your user account
4. Navigate to Privacy Policy, Account > Privacy & Security > Privacy Policy
5. Read the Privacy Policy

![alt text](4-privacy-policy.png)

### Description

The privacy policy outlines how the OWASP Juice Shop collects, uses, and protects user data. It emphasizes the importance of user privacy and provides information on the types of data collected, how it is used, and the measures taken to ensure its security. By reading the privacy policy, users can understand their rights and the responsibilities of the OWASP Juice Shop in handling their personal information.
